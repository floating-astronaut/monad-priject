MONAD | Gate · 01 / 06

Your agent's mistakes
are already your problem.
This puts a ceiling on them.

A permission layer for AI agents, live on Monad testnet.

No owner. No admin key. Not even for us — there is no upgrade path and no privileged role in the contract.

02 / 06 · the gap

Agents can plan.
They can't be trusted with a wallet.

An agent that needs to act as an economic actor either freezes and waits for a human, or runs on a human's hot wallet. Neither is acceptable.

Execution is already autonomous. Identity, authority and liability are not.

03 / 06 · the failure

Today, "autonomous"
means an unattended key.

The overnight rebalance bot glitches and asks for 10× size. A compromised agent raises its own limit. A stranger points liability at someone who never agreed.

All three are one prompt injection away, and none of them are hypothetical.

04 / 06 · the mechanism

Agency law,
written as a contract.

A principal grants authority. They are bound by acts inside it, and not bound by acts outside it.

registerAgent → consent, signed by the principal
setPolicy     → cap · allowed action · active
executeGated  → attest, or revert

Registration reverts unless the principal signs it. Liability is accepted, never assigned.

05 / 06 · the proof

Five ways
to be refused.

over the cap          → SpendCapExceeded(100, 10)
agent self-escalates  → NotPrincipal
liability assigned    → NotPrincipal
undelegated action    → ActionNotAllowed
result replayed       → ResultAlreadyAttested
unregistered caller   → AgentNotRegistered

Reproduce every one of them from your own phone, with no wallet, right now — a refusal is an eth_call, so it costs nothing. Only a yes has to be paid for. And a success is checkable too: the attestation id is recomputable from the event alone, which the site does in front of you.

06 / 06 · honest boundary

What we
will not claim.

It moves no value — amounts are policy units. It proves authorization, not that the work happened: the result hash is agent-declared.

Denials revert, so the chain records what passed, not what was blocked. Rotating an agent resets its replay set. No reputation, no post-quantum signatures, no Hedera anchoring — not built, and not designed either. We are not claiming a spec we do not have.

Register who the agent is and who is liable. Set what it may do. The chain refuses the rest.
